# Changelog

## BayesianBenter 0.0.0.9000

- Initial development version.
- [`gibbsBenter()`](https://pearce790.github.io/BayesianBenter/reference/gibbsBenter.md):
  Gibbs sampler for the Benter ranking model with exponential and
  alpha-stable augmentation; handles complete and partial (top-R)
  rankings.
- [`rBenter()`](https://pearce790.github.io/BayesianBenter/reference/rBenter.md):
  simulate complete rankings from the Benter model.
